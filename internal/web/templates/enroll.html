Safari only Configuration profiles do not download inside app browsers such as Slack or Gmail. If you opened this from a link, tap the share icon and choose Open in Safari first.
Download the profile

Then finish in Settings

  1. Tap Allow when Safari asks to download a configuration profile.
  2. Tap Close on “Profile Downloaded”.
  3. Open Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.
  4. Tap the Fledge profile, then Install, and enter your passcode.

iOS discards the download if you wait more than about eight minutes, so do it now rather than later.
